## Remaining-risk assessment for this range

1. **Highest residual risk: the evidence taxonomy.** The label system is asserted with unusual rigour in the Preface and then applied inconsistently in five of the six chapters reviewed. Because the labels are also the book's defence against staleness, the drift is a structural risk, not cosmetic. A single mechanical pass against Table 4-3 as the authority, plus a check script, would close it.
2. **Second: figure typography.** Two figures (1.2, 2.1) are well below the print floor and four more are marginal. The `boost_archify_fonts.py` BOOST mechanism already exists; what is missing is a per-figure gate in the build that measures the smallest placed font and fails the build under 8 pt.
3. **Third: the canonical scenario's availability constraint.** Ch 4 states a 99.9 % multi-region requirement and then declares it unpriced. Whatever the review of Ch 16–17 concludes, this range has already created the inconsistency; it should be resolved in the canonical box rather than in a caveat.
4. **Chapters needing another pass:** Ch 4 (tables 4-1/4-2 duplication and the 3-page prose-table) and Ch 6 (figure-6.1 caption/whitespace, table 6-1 split, S-key introduction). Ch 1 is the strongest chapter in this range and needs only the LLaMA constant fix, the duplicated sentence, and the stranded §1.8 heading.
5. **Claims requiring verification before publication:** the LLaMA-2 hidden width, all-MiniLM-L6-v2 parameter count, the 50 tok/s vLLM figure and its provenance, and every 2026 frontier constant in §3.6.1 (Qwen3.8-Flash-Next 125 B/6 B, Muse Glimmer 30B, Kimi K3 104 B/2.8 T) which I could not verify in this pass.
6. **Most likely place for a subtle undiscovered error:** the p89/p90 economy block, where three different dollar bases (managed-API list, frontier API, owned-fleet $/hr) are reconciled in prose but the labels around them are the weakest in the book.
