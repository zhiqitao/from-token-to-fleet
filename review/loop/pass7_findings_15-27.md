## FINDINGS

**Severity: HIGH | Location: Ch17 §4 "Cross-host interconnect budget" (chapter-17.md lines ~100-112) | Problem:** The per-request cross-host bandwidth `B_per-req = D / (λ_net·φ·N)` is computed and quoted as **6.25 MB/s ≈ 50 Mbps** (base case: D=5 MB, λ_net=20 ms, φ=0.1, N=4) and **12.5 MB/s ≈ 100 Mbps** (φ=0.5, D=50 MB). Both are off by **100×**: 5/(0.02×0.1×4) = **625 MB/s** (≈5 Gbps), and 50/(0.02×0.5×4) = **1,250 MB/s** (≈10 Gbps). | **Why it matters:** The stated conclusion — "well within 10 Gbps capacity" — is built on wrong arithmetic. The corrected base-case figure is ~5 Gbps, i.e. half the 10 Gbps link (2× headroom, marginal), and the high case is ~10 Gbps, right at the limit. The message that cross-host traffic is benign is only incidentally true at the base case; on the actual numbers it is far less comfortable than the prose implies. | **Recommended change:** Recompute B_per-req to 625 MB/s (≈5 Gbps) and 1,250 MB/s (≈10 Gbps), and re-frame the "well within" language (base case = 5 Gbps, ~half the link, so only ~2× headroom, not an order-of-magnitude margin).

**Severity: MEDIUM | Location: Ch18 §3.2 price table vs §8 mini-case (chapter-18.md lines 64-70 vs 150-159) | Problem:** §3.2 quotes per-1M-token prices (70B FP16 $2.50, 8-bit dense $1.25, MoE 8-bit $1.00, 4-bit GGUF $0.15 per 1M). The §8 mini-case quotes **per-token** prices (Tier3 70B FP16 $0.003/token, Tier2 $0.0015/token, Tier1 $0.0002/token) and multiplies tokens × that rate (4,000×0.0002 = $0.80). Converting the mini-case per-token rates to per-1M gives $3,000 / $1,500 / $200 per 1M — **~1,000–1,200× higher** than the §3.2 numbers for the same model classes. | **Why it matters:** A reader cannot reconcile the two sets; the same "70B FP16" tier costs either $2.50/1M (§3.2) or $3,000/1M (mini-case). The mini-case costs appear intended as $/1,000-tokens (≈$3, $1.5, $0.2 per 1K, roughly matching §3.2's per-1M ×1,000), but they are labeled and multiplied as $/token. This is a unit-basis error that inflates the mini-case cost figures and undermines the mixed-fleet economics claim. | **Recommended change:** State the mini-case prices consistently with §3.2 (either as $/1M or $/1K), correct the unit labels (e.g. "$0.003 per 1K tokens" not "$0.003/token"), and re-derive the $2.68/hr / $8,855/mo savings on the corrected basis so it reconciles with the §3.2 per-1M tables.

**Severity: MEDIUM | Location: Ch23 (chapter-23.md) — §1 (lines 11-17), §4 item 1 (line 75), §6 (line 90), vs Table 23-1 (line 60) | Problem:** The chapter alternates between "**five** numbers / five bounds" (§1 lists 5 items with latency-and-cost bundled as item 4; §4 says "all five numbers (traffic, tokens, quality, latency/cost, ops)"; §6 says "the five bounds… traffic, tokens, quality, latency, cost, ops" and then enumerates **six**) and "**six** questions" (Table 23-1 title, line 60, has six rows, splitting SLO and TCO/cost into separate rows). | **Why it matters:** The central message of the chapter — extract N numbers from the customer — has an inconsistent N. A reader is left unsure whether cost is bundled with the SLO budget or is a separate sixth bound, and the mini-case (§8) surfaces cost and latency separately. This is the axis the whole chapter is built on. | **Recommended change:** Pick one count. Since §6, Table 23-1 and the mini-case all treat cost as distinct from latency, standardize on **six** bounds and change §1 and §4 (item 1) from "five" to "six" accordingly, or explicitly say "five (with cost folded into the latency/cost budget) — but note Table 23-1 splits it into six rows."

**Severity: MEDIUM | Location: Ch17 Table 17-1 scope note (chapter-17.md line 166) | Problem:** The caption/scope note says "W ≈ 13.8 s for the **4-turn** agentic request," but the derivation in §3 (line 154) and the §8 mini-case compute W at **T=3** (three turns, 12,095-token context, 495 generated tokens). The same note calls it "the mini-case's service-time-aware figure." | **Why it matters:** The table's in-flight column for the "agentic" W is driven by the 13.8 s figure, which the book derives at T=3 (the 95th-percentile turn count it tells readers to size against). Labeling it "4-turn" mis-states the scenario and could make a reader think the fleet is sized for a 4-turn request when the arithmetic is for 3. | **Recommended change:** Change "4-turn agentic request" to "**T=3 (95th-percentile) agentic request**" (or "3-turn"), matching the mini-case and the chapter's own "size for the 95th-percentile" rule. If the 4-turn (out-of-turn-limit) W is intended, derive it separately.

**Severity: LOW | Location: Ch17 §4 line 106 and §6 "Cross-host data infrastructure" line 134 | Problem:** Base-case aggregate cross-host bandwidth is stated as "B_min ≈ 0.5 Gbps." The same formula B = φ·N·D/λ_net with φ=0.1, N=4, D=5 MB, λ_net=20 ms gives 0.1×4×(5/0.02) = 100 MB/s = **0.8 Gbps**, not 0.5 Gbps. (This also differs from §4's own aggregate formula result for the same inputs.) | **Why it matters:** Internal inconsistency in a worked arithmetic that is meant to demonstrate disciplined bandwidth provisioning; the 0.5 vs 0.8 Gbps gap is small but the derivation should produce it correctly. | **Recommended change:** Recompute to ~0.8 Gbps (or show the substitution that yields 0.5 Gbps) so §4 and §6 agree and the "10 Gbps is ample" verdict rests on the right number.

**Severity: LOW | Location: Ch17 §8 mini-case (line 154) and Ch19 §4 scope note (line 120) — agentic full service time | Problem:** The full agentic W ≈ 13.8 s is built from "**1.08 s prefill of the 12,095-token context** + ~12.4 s decode + ~0.4 s tool latency." Prefill at 12,095 tokens should be ~1.42 s (1.08 × 12,095/9,200) if it scales with input length, making W ≈ 14.2 s. Separately, the "~0.4 s tool latency" in this W is inconsistent with Ch19's same-T=3 orchestration overhead of T·L_agent ≈ 660 ms (120 ms step + 100 ms tool per turn). | **Why it matters:** The W that drives all the fleet-sizing numbers (344→552 in-flight, 141 hosts, etc.) uses a prefill that doesn't scale with the grown context and a tool-latency figure that disagrees with the chapter's own per-turn overhead. The in-flight counts and host totals are therefore slightly under-stated. | **Recommended change:** Either scale the prefill for the 12,095-token context (~1.42 s) or state explicitly that the 1.08 s is a fixed prefill representative; and reconcile the tool/orchestration term with Ch19's 660 ms figure so both chapters agree on the agentic overhead.

**Severity: LOW | Location: Ch24 §8 mini-case (line 154) vs Table 24-1 (lines 166-173) | Problem:** The mini-case states that at the point "PIES has dropped from 217 to 143," "the tool-use success rate is 0.32%." **Severity: LOW | Location: Ch15 §5 (line 70) vs §6 (line 76) — FP8 KV conversion constant | Problem:** Line 70 says "KV quantization to cut the footprint (FP8 ≈**2.5→1.3** MB/token)", while line 76 uses "FP8 KV quantization (≈**2.62 → ~1.42** MB/token, at 54% of BF16)".
