# Loop 1 Findings — Chapters 19–27 + All Back Matter

**Scope.** Built PDF `render/build/from-token-to-fleet-v20260913.pdf`, PDF pages 246–356 (body pages 228–338), i.e. Chapters 19–26, Appendix A (frontier model families), Glossary, References, Sources/Method/Reproducibility, and the Architecture Worksheet. Reviewed against `review/complete-manuscript-review-prompt.md` (TAOCP-inspired standard). Pdf page = book page + 18.

**Method.** Each page rendered at 130 dpi and the 15 figure pages at 200 dpi; every figure vision-inspected for legibility (≥8pt) / overlap / clipping / caption-split; pages scanned programmatically for literal-markdown leaks, text/table overflow vs the ~525.6pt right edge, orphan/widow and stranded headings; printed arithmetic re-derived in Python; cross-references and the evidence taxonomy compared across the Preface, the Sources chapter, and the Worksheet; the in-text `[S#]` citation keys cross-checked against the References chapter; and repository artifacts referenced by the back matter (worksheet, build.sh, sources.csv, source commit) verified.

**Verdict.** No CRITICAL findings. The chapter prose, the mini-cases, and the rendered arithmetic in ch 19–26 largely check out. The most serious issues are (a) a stale cross-reference that points the reader to "Chapter 26" for the evidence taxonomy, and (b) an unreconciled second evidence-labeling scheme (measurement-status) in the Sources chapter that the Worksheet hides and the body uses before it is defined. There are also a few back-matter coherence faults (figure/caption scale conflict, an incomplete "four-family" figure, edition-panel versioning tension) and one clean-looking copy-ready template that is split across a page break.

## Confirmation / refutation of the suspected defect classes

- **Taxonomy drift across Preface / Sources / Worksheet — PARTIALLY CONFIRMED.** The two-axis taxonomy is stated *consistently* in the Preface (p11), the Sources chapter (p346–347), and the Worksheet (p353), and the Worksheet's listing matches the Sources chapter exactly. The real problem is different: a **second** scheme (measurement-status: ANALYTICAL/ILLUSTRATIVE/MODELED/MEASURED/UNMEASURED) is defined only in the Sources chapter (p352), is used in the body before that definition (p228/p263 `[ANALYTICAL]`, p316 `[MEASURED]`), is absent from the Worksheet, reuses the `[ILLUSTRATIVE]` token, and is combined with `[LAB]` in the Appendix (p316) without an explicit relationship. Separately, Ch 11 cross-refs the taxonomy as "Chapter 26" (wrong — see MAJOR #1).
- **Glossary gaps — CONFIRMED.** KV quantization, KV offloading, prompt caching, and concurrency are load-bearing in the body/references but missing from the glossary (MODERATE #8); plus one garbled bullet (MINOR #9).
- **Citation-[S-n] mismatch — MOSTLY REFUTED, one title garbled.** The `[S1]`–`[S7]` keys in the References chapter map cleanly to the body's usage: S1 Orca (continuous batching), S2 PagedAttention/vLLM, S3 prefix caching (SGLang/RadixAttention), S4 P/D disaggregation (Splitwise/DistServe/Mooncake), S5 DFlash (speculative decoding), S6 vLLM FP8-KV blog, S7 vLLM KV-offloading blog. No key-number mismatch. One naming fault: the Ch 11 `[S3]` descriptor garbles the SGLang title (MINOR #11). Also note `[S#]` keys are not used in the ch 19–27 body (they are concentrated in ch 6–11 / 14–15), which is appropriate since they are mechanism references. The References chapter correctly adds S7 and distinguishes S6 from S7.
- **You-address — CONFIRMED (minor).** A handful of direct second-person slips in an otherwise third-person, impersonal book (MINOR #10).
- **Edition / release-manifest wording — CONFIRMED tension.** The "single cutoff" + versioned-addendum + "A later content change requires a new build identifier" rules do not reconcile (MODERATE #5).

## Most serious finding

The highest-value single fix is the **MAJOR #1 stale cross-reference** ("the evidence taxonomy of Chapter 26") combined with the **MAJOR #2 unreconciled second evidence scheme**: together they mean a reader pointed to the book's evidence-labeling system cannot reliably find or apply one of the two systems the body actually uses. Both are in the reference/evidence layer that the preface and the whole two-axis-labeling promise rest on.
