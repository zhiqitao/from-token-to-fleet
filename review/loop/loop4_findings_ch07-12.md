## C. Focus item 3 — the deep-review questions, answered

- **§50 "is this the best way to teach?"** — most of the range is genuinely strong: ch8's "Three different bounds — keep them apart" callout, its per-host-vs-fleet scope note, §8.8's four-cause profile-then-decide mini-case, ch9's lower-bound discipline ("note the word lower bound"), ch11's mechanism→resource→trade-off table and "Never conclude 'X is the go-to engine'", ch7's residency-floor/practical-requirement split are model sections. The teaching gaps that remain are concentrated in ch12's gate vocabulary (D1), ch9's mini-case (D3), and ch12's economics (D5).
- **§19 "does each section/figure/example earn its place?"** — no cut candidate found in pp107–177; every section establishes a prerequisite, quantifies a constraint, or exposes a trade-off. Ch7's fine-tuning contrast and ch9's training all-reduce both earn their place by preventing unit/registry imports later (and ch9's mini-case explicitly re-scopes to inference).
- **§7 prefill/decode causal model** — **coherent and not overgeneralized.** The regimes are stated as scoped first-order facts everywhere they appear: "at the canonical long-prompt operating point prefill is compute-bound; at low batch decode is memory-bound" (p129); "decode intensity ≈ batch size … compute-bound decode needs batch ≈ 295" (p131/133); the shard-invariance note (p131); the per-GPU-roofline scope note on Fig 8.2; §8.8 explicitly attacks the misuse ("The temptation is to reach for a roofline and 'prove' prefill is memory-bound. That reasoning is wrong here"). TTFT↔prefill and TPOT↔decode are tied to their creating subsystems (§8.3 scope note, §11.4 telemetry). No universal-law phrasing survives in range.
- **§10 optimization-as-diagnosis** — **follows symptom→bottleneck→mechanism→trade-off→when-it-hurts.** Continuous batching (discrete-batch bubbles → Orca admit/evict), PagedAttention (24.1 GB contiguous fragmentation → blocks → "more concurrency, not faster single-request math"), prefix caching (repeated 8K re-prefill → Radix/APC → hit-rate dependency), KV quant (KV floor binds, not bandwidth → "a memory lever, orthogonal"), P/D (opposite phase resources → pools → "premature for the canonical single host"; §11.5 names the failure case; §11.8 declines it). Chunked prefill and speculative decoding live in ch15 (out of range); in range spec-dec appears only as a scoped common mistake (DFlash numbers explicitly non-portable).
- **ch12 candidate reconciliation** — **verdicts are intellectually clean (no forced consistency): figure, table, prose, and closing all say the same thing, and the figure's own OUTCOME box carries the "~5-host" condition rather than a bare verdict.** What is *not* clean is the level below: the chapter never fixes which three gates define candidacy, and the ECON gate the figure names is never evaluated (D1, D5). Consistency was achieved at verdict level, not at method level.
