## C. Focus item 3 — the deep-review questions, answered

- **§50 "is this the best way to teach?"** — the range's strongest teaching units are unchanged and still strong: ch8's "Three different bounds — keep them apart" callout and its per-host-vs-fleet scope note; §8.3.2's "at 32K +60% … at 128K it dominates" (the correct form of F7's claim); ch9's lower-bound discipline plus §9.3's flat-vs-ring reconciliation in one paragraph (0.16/5.6/56 s flat vs 0.27/6.1/98 s ring, with "note the word lower bound"); ch11's mechanism→resource→trade-off table; ch12's now-single gate vocabulary with a live elimination rule and an explicit economic deferral. The teaching defects that remain are concentrated in **captions and figure annotations rather than prose**: Fig 7.2's Scope line states the wrong number for the curve it captions (F1), Fig 8.2's central chart has its own annotation crossed by its own curve (F5), and Fig 9.1's legend adds a tier the book's own table denies (F3). Prose-level, ch7's KV-quantization reduction (F2) teaches a bound that ch12 then violates.
- **§19 "does each section/figure/example earn its place?"** — no cut candidate in pp108–179; every section establishes a prerequisite, quantifies a constraint, or exposes a trade-off. Two figures do not yet earn their *ink*: Fig 12.1 is never pointed at from prose (F12) and Fig 10.1's color key carries no information a grayscale reader can use (F16) — both are fix-in-place, not cut.
- **§7 prefill/decode causal model** — **coherent and not overgeneralized.** Regimes are scoped first-order facts everywhere they appear; the shard-invariance pointer (p132/p158 → Ch 6) resolves correctly (ch6 pp101/103 carry 5.6 TB/s, 0.70 TB/s and the "intensity is low and batching raises it" cause); TTFT↔prefill and TPOT↔decode stay tied to their creating subsystems. No universal-law phrasing survives.
- **§10 optimization-as-diagnosis** — **follows symptom→bottleneck→mechanism→trade-off→when-it-hurts** for all five in-range mechanisms (continuous batching, PagedAttention, prefix caching, KV quant, P/D); §11.8 declines P/D for the canonical host and §11.5 names the failure case. The one structural slip is §10.3.2's `Ttp` label (F11).
- **ch12 candidate reconciliation** — **now clean at method level, not just verdict level.** One gate set across six artifacts, an elimination rule that fires with a concrete example, economics deferred with its consequence stated and reconciled at ~10×, all three candidates labelled "conditional" with an open gate in figure, table, prose, and closing. This is the loop's biggest improvement.
