## FINDINGS

**Severity: LOW | Location: Ch20 §8, toward end (chapter-20.md lines ~117-119; PDF p.208 line 9019) | Problem:** The heading "**Table 20-1** — Fleet QPS and latency vs. host count, batch size, and scheduling overhead." is an **orphan caption**: it is followed directly by a hard page break and then the Fig 20.1 figure (figures/fig-20-2001.png), with no table content under the Table 20-1 label anywhere in the chapter. In the built PDF the caption sits alone at the bottom of one page and the figure appears on the next. | **Why it matters:** A numbered table with no body reads as a broken/empty artifact; the reader is left looking for a data table that does not exist, and the cross-downstream references (if any) to "Table 20-1" land on a figure. | **Recommended change:** Either supply the intended table content (the caption at minimum implies a host-count/QPS/latency table), or delete the orphan "Table 20-1" label and let the figure stand on its own as Fig 20.1 (and renumber any downstream reference accordingly).

**Severity: LOW | Location: Ch26 §8 Step 4 — "Tab 26.1" cross-reference (chapter-26.md line ~99; PDF p.235 line 11035) | Problem:** Step 4 of the capstone mini-case says the pattern instance "is recorded in the Patterns Library as **Tab 26.1**, ready for the next fleet expansion." The pattern-instance table in §8 is actually **Table 26-2** ("Pattern Instance: Sharded RAG Serving"); Table 26-1 is the §4 "Pattern measurement targets and observed values" table. After the PASS-7 renumbering (which swapped the two table labels so 26-1 precedes 26-2), this prose reference was not updated and still points to 26.1. | **Why it matters:** The reader following "Tab 26.1" is misdirected to the measurement-target table instead of the pattern-instance table, breaking the step-4→Table link in the capstone. | **Recommended change:** Change "Tab 26.1" to "**Table 26-2**."

**Severity: LOW | Location: Ch26 §2.1 workload-to-strategy matrix, "Fine-tuning" row (chapter-26.md line ~33; PDF p.234 line 10835) | Problem:** The strategy cell reads "**Precised-optimizer** (LoRA/QLoRA) before full FT; gradient checkpointing." "Precised-optimizer" is a malformed/typo phrase — presumably intended "precision-aware optimizer," "precision-optimized," or simply "precise optimizer." | **Why it matters:** It is an obvious typo in a table that is meant to look authoritative and copy-safe; it reads as an editing artifact in a convergence pass. | **Recommended change:** Rewrite to "Precision-aware optimizer (LoRA/QLoRA) before full FT; gradient checkpointing."

**Severity: LOW | Location: Ch18 §3.1 hardware-capacity table, "70B MoE FP16 (2/16 experts)" row (chapter-18.md line ~51; PDF p.204 line 7892) | Problem:** The "# fit on 8×H100" column gives **"0 (same as dense)"** for 70B MoE FP16, but the row immediately above ("70B dense FP16") gives **"1"** for the same 140 GB FP16 residency, with the note "weights + KV fit in the 640 GB pool; ~2 GPUs hold the weights." Two models with identical 140 GB weight residency are labeled 1 and "0 (same as dense)" — the "0" contradicts both the "same as dense" qualifier and the adjacent row. | **Why it matters:** The capacity-mapping table is the basis for the mixed-fleet placement arithmetic; a reader cannot tell whether a 70B MoE FP16 fits (1) or does not (0) on the host, and "0 (same as dense)" is internally self-contradictory. | **Recommended change:** Make the MoE FP16 row consistent with the dense row (e.g. "1 (weights + KV fit in the 640 GB pool, same as dense)"), or state explicitly that it does not fit and why, without the "same as dense" qualifier.

**Severity: LOW | Location: Ch18 §8 mini-case monthly figures (chapter-18.md lines ~159, ~161; PDF p.205 lines 8141, 8144) | Problem:** The mini-case reports "**Total hourly cost: $2.68** → **$1,945 / month**" and — for the single-70B-FP16 comparison — "5,000 tokens/hr × $0.003 = $15/hr → **$10,800 / month**. **Savings: $8,855 / month**." These monthly figures do not reconcile on a single month length: $2.68/hr × 720 hr = **$1,929.60 ≈ $1,930** (not $1,945); $15/hr × 720 hr = $10,800 (which is consistent with 720 hr); and the savings should then be $10,800 − $1,930 ≈ **$8,870** (not $8,855, which equals $10,800 − $1,945 using the inconsistent mixed figure; $1,945÷$2.68 ≈ 725.7 hr, matching neither 720 nor 730). | **Why it matters:** The headline "savings" of the three-tier mini-case is the chapter's economics takeaway; it is built on a mixed figure ($1,945) and a savings figure ($8,855) that are each inconsistent with the hourly basis and with each other, so the absolute monthly numbers do not reproduce from the stated per-hour rates on the canonical 720-hr month. | **Recommended change:** Recompute on the canonical 720-hr month consistently: mixed ≈ $1,930/mo, single ≈ $10,800/mo, savings ≈ $8,870/mo (≈82%), and update the two displayed figures ($1,945 → ~$1,930; $8,855 → ~$8,870).)

**Severity: LOW | Location: Ch24 §8 mini-case vs Table 24-1 (chapter-24.md line ~154 vs lines 166-173; PDF p.215 line 10284 vs line 10316) | Problem:** The mini-case scenario states that "**PIES has dropped from 217 to 143**" and "**The tool‑use success rate is 0.32%**" at that point. Table 24-1 pairs PIES=143 (Cycle 2) with **tool‑use success 0.44%**, and gives 0.32% only at PIES=138 (Cycle 3). The mini-case figure is therefore inconsistent with the summary table at the same cycle. | **Why it matters:** This was flagged in PASS-7 (finding P7-N) and marked FIXED, but the source and the built PDF **still** contain "0.32%" at PIES=143 — the claimed fix did not land. The mini-case telemetry and the summary table continue to disagree on which cycle's tool-use rate is being quoted, in a chapter whose whole point is reproducible metric discipline. | **Recommended change:** Confirm the fix truly landed: set the mini-case tool-use figure to **0.44%** at PIES=143/Cycle 2 (matching Table 24-1), or explicitly label the 0.32% as the later Cycle-3 value, and re-render the PDF.
