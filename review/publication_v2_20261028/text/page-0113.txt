The figure shows one group’s geometry: 8 query heads funnel into a single shared K/V head. 
Because GQA caches only one K/V per group (8 K/V per token) instead of one per query head (64 
K/V per token), the per-token KV falls ~8× — from ~2.62 MB/token (full MHA) to ~0.33 MB/token. 
That is the takeaway: many query heads can share a few K/V heads, and the cache shrinks by exactly 
the 8:1 head ratio. Head count is the real dial: substitute the model’s KV-head count into 2 × layers 
× KV_heads × head_dim × bytes.
