6.3.4. Reading Telemetry Into a Decision
A concrete dashboard-read → decision trace. We see: p95 TTFT climbing (serving), KV cache hit 
ratio low (resource: memory), and prefill queue depth growing (serving). Workload is unchanged. 
Reading up the chain: low prefix-cache hit means many independent 9.2K prefixes are being prefill-
processed from scratch on the ~40 rps peak — each ~1.29 PFLOP saturating FLOPs. The decision: 
enable prefix / RadixAttention-style caching (SGLang) or Automatic Prefix Caching (vLLM) so shared 
retrieved-context prefixes are reused instead of re-prefilled, cutting effective prefill FLOPs and TTFT. 
[S3][1P][FACT] Prefix caching is a compute-saving move, not a latency tuning — and the telemetry 
told us that.
