figure 2: Reading the book two ways. A (left): layer-by-layer, Part I → VI, building the substrate then 
the discipline. B (right): question-by-question, a set of independent navigation prompts — each maps 
one reader’s question (token → cost, fits host?, which phase binds?, 1 host or fleet?, who serves it?, 
is it the right call?) to the chapter that answers it. These question-to-chapter links are starting points, 
not complete coverage: a single question often spans several chapters, and the decision-justification 
chapters (Ch 22 for the design loop, Ch 25 for the recorded Architecture Decision Record) carry the 
reasoning that the navigation prompt points toward. The two paths are independent; whichever fits 
the reader’s question, each chapter still supplies a derived quantity that feeds a decision.
