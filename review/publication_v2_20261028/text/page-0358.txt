The Architecture Worksheet
How to use this
Every chapter of this book turns a vague ask into a defensible number. This worksheet is the reverse: 
a single reusable worksheet that applies the whole loop to the architect’s own workload. Fill it in 
row by row; each row is a decision in Chapters 1–26. When a row is empty, that is precisely the gap 
to investigate next. The canonical workload box (Chapter 4) shows what a fully-filled row looks like; 
the two derived rates most often needed are given under its table.
All quantities are the architect’s own — traceable, verifiable, and stated in the evidence tax­
onomy (provenance: [1P] first-party, [canonical scenario] the book’s own workload, [2◦] reputable 
secondary, [LAB] home-lab, [ILLUSTRATIVE] worked-example; epistemic status: FACT / DERIVED / 
ASSUMPTION / HYPOTHESIS / UNRESOLVED; measurement status — the third, orthogonal axis 
— ANALYTICAL / ILLUSTRATIVE / MODELED / MEASURED / UNMEASURED). See the Sources, 
Method, and Reproducibility chapter for the full three-axis definition.
