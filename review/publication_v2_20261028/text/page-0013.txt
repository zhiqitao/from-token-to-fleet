The architect's decision loop
figure 1: The architect’s decision loop — the book’s spine. A vague requirement is carried through 
workload characterization, constraints, candidate architectures, benchmarking, bottleneck analysis, 
TCO, and a final red-team challenge before an architecture is committed. Stage 03 (“Validate & 
Defend”) produces a provisional recommendation — the candidate that survives benchmarking and 
TCO — and stage 04 (“Commit”) subjects it to the red-team challenge before the final choice is 
recorded; the recommendation is therefore provisional until it has been challenged. Two loops are 
drawn: the inner (green, left) returns a failed benchmark or TCO validation from “Validate & Defend” 
back to “Design & size” to re-derive the candidate; the outer (red, right) returns from “Commit” to 
“Understand” when the committed architecture reveals a requirements revision is needed.
