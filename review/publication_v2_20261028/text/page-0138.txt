The bandwidth each collective consumes depends on three factors: the data volume (bytes), the 
number of participating nodes (N), and the interconnect’s effective bandwidth in the presence of 
contention. A 70B FP16 model is 140 GB, and its FP16 gradient is the same size — the §9.3 worked 
example all-reduces that 140 GB gradient tensor. An all-reduce of a 140 GB tensor across 8 GPUs on 
a single node moves 140 GB over NVLink; across 8 nodes connected by InfiniBand it moves 140 GB 
(or more, depending on the algorithm) over the fabric. The same 140 GB over a 25 Gb/s Ethernet link 
(≈ 2.5 GB/s practical-effective) takes ≈ 56 s — about 350× the ≈ 0.16 s over a 900 GB/s NVLink (§9.2, 
§9.3).
