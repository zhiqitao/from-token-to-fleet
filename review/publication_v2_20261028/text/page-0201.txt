Two cautions. First, do not read the table as “technique X fixes symptom Y”: the same symptom 
can come from two bottlenecks (high TPOT with a bandwidth-saturated GPU is different from high 
TPOT with a capacity-capped GPU), so the evidence column matters as much as the symptom. Second, 
the last two rows (P/D disaggregation, interconnect tuning) are the expensive, structural levers — 
they are worth testing only after the cheaper in-kernel and in-scheduler mechanisms have been 
measured and shown insufficient, because they change capacity, orchestration, and cost, not just a 
kernel constant.
