These numbers illustrate the scale: moving from NVLink to InfiniBand adds ~22× latency, and 
forcing the same all-reduce over Ethernet adds ~350× latency. In a training job where 100 all-reduce 
steps run per iteration, the NVLink path adds ~27 s of communication per iteration, the InfiniBand 
path adds ~610 s, and the Ethernet path adds ~9,800 s. The latter two would effectively serialize the 
training, turning a minutes-long job into an hours- or days-long one.
