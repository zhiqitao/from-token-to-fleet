Average arrival rate
~10 rps [canonical scenario]
[ASSUMPTION] declared traffic input 
(2,000 users × one request ~every 200 s ≈ 
10 rps) — declared, not derived 
(walk-through in §4.3). Time basis: this 
is the rate over the active 
business-hours window (an 8-hour × 
260-workday schedule), not 24×7 
wall-clock. Over 24×7 the same 10 rps 
would extrapolate to ~25.9M 
requests/month, or ~12,960 
requests/user/month for 2,000 users — 
an implausible all-day activity level for 
an internal enterprise document-Q&A 
tool. So read 10 rps as an intentionally 
stress-oriented busy-period average 
(a capacity ceiling to design against), 
and price request-volume economics 
from the active-hours schedule rather 
than from raw wall-clock 
multiplication.
