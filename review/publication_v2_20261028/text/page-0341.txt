figure A.4: Where should intelligence live? The horizontal bands are a design choice (which layer 
owns which capability), not a ranking of superiority: a lower band is not “better” or “worse” than a 
higher one, and the boundaries shift with the workload, the trust model, and the cost/latency budget. 
Read each band as “what we put here and why,” not “what is best here.”
