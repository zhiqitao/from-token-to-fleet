The agent loop: a four-state state machine
resolved → final answer; no resolution within the turn limit → stopped
figure 19.1: The agent loop as a four-state state machine (Plan, Execute, Observe, Decide) with a 
loop-back to Plan and the terminal outcomes final answer and stopped. Exit conditions: the loop 
terminates on (a) a final answer (the model decides no more tool calls are needed), (b) a budget 
cap (max turns or max tokens reached), (c) a tool error that cannot be recovered, (d) a policy stop 
(guardrail or safety rule triggered), or (e) human approval required. The Decide state evaluates these 
guard conditions each turn; the loop-back to Plan is bounded by the turn budget, so an accidental 
infinite loop is prevented by the budget cap, not by the model’s judgment alone.
