Same message as Fig 7.1’s GQA line, taken to the frontier: the per-token KV and FLOP “constants” of 
Chapter 7/8 are not universal — they are the canonical full-MHA conservative upper-bound teaching 
model, which hybrid attention re-architects down at the mechanism. Each row is a model’s 
vendor-reported ratio of its own stated reference (e.g. DeepSeek V4 vs V3.2); they do not share a common 
denominator, so they must not be read as percentages of a single baseline. The canonical 70B dense 
full-MHA model is the book’s teaching floor, not the denominator for these percentages. An architect 
who re-derives them per candidate model avoids both over- (sizing a 7% KV model as if it were 100%) 
and under-provisioning. Values are vendor-published [1P], not yet independently reprofiled on a serving 
stack (see A.7).
