[ILLUSTRATIVE][DERIVED]: tokens/s per dollar for generation. On‑demand H100 list price

Economics unit note (reconciliation with Ch. 4 and Ch. 15). Both chapters use 
the same on-demand price basis: ~$2.50/hr per H100 → ~$20/hr per 8×H100 host. Two 
things must be kept straight. First, units: this chapter expresses tokens per dollar-hour 
(token-rate × 3600 s ÷ hourly cost), while Chapter 4 expresses tokens per second 
per dollar (token-rate ÷ per-second cost); convert by ÷3600. Second, throughput basis: 
the ~50 tok/s used here is a deliberately conservative single-stream / low-batch 
generation rate adopted to illustrate the per-dollar method, not a claim about the 
host’s batched serving throughput. Chapter 15’s measured-range figure (raw batch-1 ≈ 
8,000 tok/s rising to ~35,000–70,000 tok/s under FlashAttention + continuous batching) is 
the multi-request serving throughput of the same host. The per-dollar figures therefore 
scale up substantially at batch, and the 9,000 tokens/dollar-hour figure should be read 
as a floor set by the single-stream assumption, not as the host’s economic optimum.
