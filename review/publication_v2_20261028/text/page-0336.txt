A.7. What This Means for the Handbook’s Canonical Arithmetic
The book’s canonical scenario is a 70B dense FP16, ~9.2K-token RAG workload on 8×H100. The 2026 
frontier does not invalidate it — it refines the interpretation an architect should carry:
• The KV constant (Ch7: ~2.62 MB/token FP16; ~1.3 MB/token 8-bit) is the canonical full-MHA 
conservative upper-bound teaching model. (The 8-bit figure is the raw tensor byte-halving 
bound: ~1.31 MB/token, exactly 50% of BF16, which is what the fleet-sizing chapters use.
