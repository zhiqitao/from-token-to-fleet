figure 22.1: Prefill vs decode scaling as context grows; two panels, different units, not directly 
comparable. Takeaway: the quadratic attention term overtakes the parameterized-linear term at L* 
= N/(2·n_layers·d) = 70 × 10⁹ / (2 × 80 × 8,192) ≈ 53.4K tokens for the canonical 80-layer, hidden-
8,192 reference geometry (Ch 7) — not yet dominant at 32K. Scope: LEFT = prefill per request (2NL, 
quadratic 4·n_layers·L²·d, total); RIGHT = decode per token — the plotted 2N ≈ 1.4e-4 PFLOP/token 
is the parameter term only, flat across context (full per-token decode also includes attention over 
the growing KV cache, not shown).
For standard full attention, attention work scales quadratically with sequence length while the 
MLP/projection portion stays roughly linear, so total prefill compute becomes increasingly dominated by 
the quadratic attention term as context grows (the 9.2K canonical point is marked). Name the ratios 
explicitly: at 9.2K the quadratic attention term is ~17% of the parameterized-linear 2NL term 
(so it is still subordinate), at 128K it is ~2.4× the linear term (attention relative to linear), and total 
prefill FLOPs at 128K are ~3.4× the 2NL baseline (linear + quadratic, i.e. 1 + 2.4). Prefill is 
compute-bound (~1.29 PFLOP/request → ~1.19 PFLOPS required at the ~1.08 s budget vs H100 ~0.989 
PFLOPS peak). The decode series is FLOPs per generated token, and is not literally flat: attending over an 
increasingly long KV cache carries context-length-dependent work and traffic, so per-token decode cost 
grows with generated-token index/context (relegated to a narrow band here; see Chapter 8). Note the 
aggregation units differ per series: the two prefill series are FLOPs per request (whole-context), the 
decode series is FLOPs per generated token (~140 GFLOP/token, independent of context length at the 
decoding step considered) — this is why the y-axis is labeled per series (prefill: PFLOP/request; decode: 
PFLOP/token). The two regimes remain deliberately separate: decode lands in the bandwidth-bound 
regime at low batch (Ch6), prefill in the compute-bound regime at long prompt (Ch8) — each an 
operating-point property, not an identity.
