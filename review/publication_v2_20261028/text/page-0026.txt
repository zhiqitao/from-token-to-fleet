How a token travels. The token is the interface between a human’s request and the machine’s arithmetic: 
carved by the tokenizer, embedded into a vector, attended against past context, and remembered in the 
KV cache. This is a conceptual abstraction of one forward pass, not the full transformer: each layer also 
runs MLP/projection, normalization, and residual paths, and the caption’s caveats apply — the full 
mechanism inventory is Ch. 3, the arithmetic Ch. 7. (Concept; the iterative autoregressive loop and the 
read-side of the persistent KV state are shown in Fig 2.1.)
