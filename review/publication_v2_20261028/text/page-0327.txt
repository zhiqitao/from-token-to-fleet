figure 26.2: Pattern Application Diagram: the Pattern-Measurement-Feedback Loop applied to the 
capstone scenario, closing on FACT → DERIVED → PATTERN. Takeaway: the three patterns 
compose to mean TTFT ~180 ms and p99 TTFT ~250 ms (−44% vs the ~450 ms uncached baseline, 
not a blended average) at ~48% lower generation cost per query. Scope: full arithmetic in §26.8;

saving (earlier ~55%); the capstone runs one configuration — 35% cache, p99 TTFT ~250 ms — stated 
identically in §26.3, §26.8, and Table 26-2.
