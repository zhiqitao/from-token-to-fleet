(Figures are worked-example estimates — a Q4 2026 illustrative pricing scenario, not market 
quotes — and treat as [ILLUSTRATIVE][DERIVED], to be re-priced before budgeting. These are 
scenario inputs the architect chooses and re-prices, not assertions governed by the 2026-08-30 evidence 
cutoff; they do not claim to be a source available at that date. The fleet requirement ~20 hosts (peak) 
/ ~5 hosts (avg) is from the canonical Ch17 sizing; FP8 KV cuts the KV-bound fleet to ~10 (the true 
peak fleet stays ~20, the throughput bound, which is independent of KV size). GQA operating 
configuration: the ~20-host figure assumes the canonical full-MHA KV constant (2.62 MB/token). 
A 2026 production model with grouped-query attention (GQA) uses roughly 8× less KV per token, 
which raises the per-host KV-residency ceiling C by ~8× and cuts the KV-bound fleet to on the 
order of ~3 hosts at the same 40 rps. That ~3-host figure assumes per-host throughput also 
scales ~8× with C (a KV/memory-bound host); the throughput bound (⌈40/2.0⌉ ≈ 20 hosts) is 
independent of KV size and still applies if the host is compute/bandwidth-bound — so the 
true fleet is max(N_throughput, N_KV), the larger of the two, not the KV bound alone. The 
full-MHA number is the conservative teaching case; own the 6–7× gap explicitly rather than quoting 
~20 hosts for a GQA deployment.)
