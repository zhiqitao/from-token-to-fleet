So the canonical workload’s peak requires roughly 20 hosts (FP16 KV, full utilization; 29 working 
hosts at the 70% utilization target — 30 total with N−1), and even the average needs ~5 — a fleet, not 
one box. (FP8 KV roughly halves the KV-bound fleet to ~10 hosts at peak — C ≈ 35.0, so ⌈344/35.0⌉
= 10, matching Ch 17 — but the true peak fleet stays ~20, the throughput bound ⌈40/2.0⌉, which is 
independent of KV size; the fleet is max(N_throughput, N_KV).) Every TCO figure below is scaled 
to this fleet requirement; pricing a single host would understate the cost by roughly 5–20×.
What kind of number is ~2.0 req/s? The per-host rate C_KV / W ≈ 17.5 / 8.6 ≈ 2.0 
req/s is an upper-order planning estimate under a particular concurrency, timing, and cache geometry 
— not an observed service rate. It is an average-in-system relation, not a measured goodput. The three 
quantities behind N_hosts = max(N_throughput, N_KV, N_SLO) are three different models 
that must be derived and measured separately, and the formula is only as strong as the weakest 
one:
• N_KV (KV-memory occupancy ceiling) — how many requests reside in KV cache given the 
host’s usable HBM and the per-token KV footprint. This is an arithmetic allocation bound (the 
~17.5 figure).
• N_throughput (phase capacity) — how many requests the host can complete per second 
given the service time. The ~8.6 s service time is an uncontended request-level estimate, and 
C/W treats it as fixed.
• N_SLO (queueing / goodput) — the number of hosts needed to hold p95/p99 TTFT, TPOT, 
and success rate under concurrent arrival. Little’s law relates average in-system work to 
elapsed time under stability; it does not independently prove a throughput ceiling or 
a p99 latency. At concurrency ~17, TTFT and TPOT can differ substantially from the isolated 
~8.6 s used to obtain C/W, because batch efficiency, per-request duration, and interference are 
all coupled to load.
So the 20-host figure is a scenario-specific bound contingent on the 8.6-second service 
time remaining valid under concurrent execution, not a deployment-validated estimate. Before 
budgeting against it, measure λ_goodput per host at the target p95/p99 TTFT, TPOT, and success 
rate on the actual workload, and re-derive N_hosts. (See the falsification protocol in Ch 17.)
