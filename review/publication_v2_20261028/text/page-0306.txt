figure 24.1: Red Team / Green Team cycle. The loop (probe → guardrails → metrics → green team) is 
operational, but it needs decision gates to be actionable: (1) a release-blocking severity criterion 
(e.g. any P0/P1 finding blocks promotion; P2/P3 are tracked but non-blocking); (2) test-corpus 
ownership (a named team maintains the probe corpus and adds new cases from each red-team run); 
(3) regression thresholds (a previously-passing probe that now fails is a regression and blocks 
release). Without these, the cycle is a ritual, not a gate.
