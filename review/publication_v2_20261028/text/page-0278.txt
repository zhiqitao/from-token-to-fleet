21.9. Transition to Part VI
Up to this point we have built the machine. Parts I–V traced one continuous engagement: a token 
enters, we price it, we size the model and memory, we measure its compute and bandwidth, we 
parallelize and serve it, we benchmark it, we price the fleet, we route and agentify it, and finally we 
industrialize its delivery in the AI Factory. The reader on the far side of Chapter 21 can name the 
workload, size the residency, place the roofline, and gate a promotion.
What remains is the question the whole book has been circling: how does an architect repeatedly 
make these decisions under uncertainty? The mechanisms are now in hand — token accounting, the 
KV constant, arithmetic intensity, batching, disaggregation, the factory gate. Part VI turns from what 
the system contains to how an architect reasons. The next chapters do not add a new subsystem; 
they formalize the loop that has quietly run through every chapter so far, make its canonical decision 
flow explicit, and leave behind a worksheet an architect can keep beside the keyboard.
