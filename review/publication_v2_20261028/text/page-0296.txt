Red Team / Green Team process is not a one-time audit; it is an ongoing practice embedded in the 
deployment lifecycle.
24.2. Mental Model
Red Team → Probes →
Exposure Surface → Guardrails →
Metrics → Green Team →
Harden → Reduce → Loop

The Green Team tracks each probe’s outcome as a FACT (observed, verifiable event), DERIVED 
(computed from lower‑level data), or HYPOTHESIS (unverified but testable claim). This axis ensures 
that every reported metric traces back to an observable anchor.
