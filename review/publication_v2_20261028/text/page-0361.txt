Following up
Keep one sheet per significant architecture decision, and re-check it when the workload’s numbers 
change. A machine-readable (Markdown) companion to this worksheet ships in the repository 
(render/architecture_worksheet.md), so a practitioner can copy, fill, and version it alongside 
the corresponding ADR rather than transcribing the printed page. The worksheet’s sequence — 
requirement, workload, model, compute/memory, architecture, decision, validation & operations — 
is the same loop Chapter 22 makes explicit, and the same reason the Patterns Library in Chapter 26 
exists: a decision recorded with its evidence can be reused, while a decision recorded without it must 
be re-derived every time. Rows 7–8 close the loop by pinning what was measured, at what load, the 
economics and failure policy assumed, and when it must be re-verified.
