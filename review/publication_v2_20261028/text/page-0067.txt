Availability topology — UNPRICED / NOT VALIDATED in the canonical cost arithmetic. 
The canonical workload’s operational constraint and SLA (this box: Multi-region deployment 
(active-active), 99.9% availability) state the requirement; the follow-on TCO and fleet arith­
metic in Ch 16–17 prices a single-region deployment with N−1 single-host failover, not multi-
region active-active. These are different provisioning problems: multi-region active-active re­
quires cross-region capacity duplication, region-failure tolerance, data locality and traffic routing, and 
consistent state/data replication across regions — none of which is priced or validated anywhere in 
this book’s cost arithmetic. The canonical cost figures (Ch 16 $/1K req, Ch 17 host counts) therefore 
understate the cost of the canonical workload’s own stated 99.9% availability requirement. 
Treat the TCO/fleet totals as single-region, N−1-only numbers, and add a separate multi-region 
capacity + replication cost line before the availability SLA can be considered met.
Throughout this book the SI convention applies: K = 1,000 and M = 1,000,000 for token counts. 
9.2K tokens therefore means 9,200 tokens, and 32K means 32,000. This matches the 2.62 MB/token 
figure, the KV residency totals (24.1 GB at 9,200 tokens; 83.9 GB at 32K), and the 128K figure (128,000 
tokens → 335.5 GB).
