A.9. The Frontier Question: Where Should Intelligence Live?
The most useful lens for an AI Solution Architect in 2026 is not “which attention mechanism does 
the model card list?” but where the intelligence and the compute budget should be placed:
The placement ladder. Every host layer that can carry intelligence is a place the architect may 
choose to push capability or cost; this book gives the arithmetic and decision framework for each 
(Ch3/7/8 weights & attention, Ch10 MoE routing, Ch19/21 post-training, Ch8/20 test-time, Ch19/24 
tools, Ch17–20 runtime, Ch18/20 fleet). [ILLUSTRATIVE][DERIVED]
