## 复算

- 8,000 ÷ 50 = **160×**
- 若 8,000 真是 batch-1 decode: 每步整模型读 140 GB ⇒ 140×10⁹ B × 8,000 = 1.12×10¹⁵ B/s = **1,120 TB/s**，而 8×H100 聚合 HBM = 8 × 3.35 = 26.8 TB/s ⇒ **41.8× 不可能**
- 规范 batch-1 decode: TPOT 预算 25 ms/token（Ch2:84/:94）⇒ **40 tok/s**；Ch5/6 示意 50 tok/s ⇒ batch-1 decode = ~40–50 tok/s
- 8,000 ≈ 9,200 ÷ 1.08 s = **8,518 ≈ 8.5K** = 单请求 prefill 主导速率（Ch4:256 明确: "the ~8.5K tok/s single-request prefill rate, 9.2K ÷ 1.08 s — that is the rate for one request monopolizing a host"）⇒ 8,000 应标为 **prefill 主导的单机 raw 速率**，不是 batch-1 decode
