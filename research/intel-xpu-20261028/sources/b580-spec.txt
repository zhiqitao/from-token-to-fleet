## Built for 1440p gaming

Play many of the latest games at 60+ FPS in 1440p resolution.1

## Smoother experiences

Faster, more fluid gameplay with a combination of Intel® XeSS 2 upscaling technology and frame generation.

## Faster responsiveness

A hyper-connected gaming experience with Intel® Xe Low Latency (Intel XeLL), which reduces lag and input delays.

## Game with Intel® XeSS

Get up to 2x performance with high image quality by enabling AI-powered Intel XeSS.1

## Powerful AI engines

Unlock new AI experiences with up to 233 TOPS of AI engine performance for content creation, real-time AI chat, editing, and upscaled gaming.2

## Generative AI-ready

Experiment with AI creation, such as editing effects and text-to-image applications, all easily accessible through Intel® AI Playground.3

## Get an AI-edge in both work and play 4

Advance your creation and play capabilities with exciting new next-gen AI tools and technologies – including upscaled visuals, increased gaming performance, and generative AI capabilities.

### Faster video editing

Dual media transcoder that enables up to 81% faster video exporting.1

### Higher bandwidth

The advanced Xe media engine utilizes parallel cores to speed up editing and significantly higher bandwidth creation.

### Cross-codec support

Support for the most popular media codecs for worry-free image and video exporting, including AV1 encode and decode capability.
