 FAQs

 
 What is LTX-2.5?
 
 



 LTX-2.5 is the newest version of LTX, an open-weights world model for video generation. It's built as a stronger foundation, not just a new tool, that teams can build on, fine-tune, and deploy themselves.





 
 What's new in LTX-2.5?
 
 



 It adds a better video decoder for cleaner motion, native multi-shot scenes that stay consistent across cuts, stronger prompt understanding, automatic clip duration, beta precise video editing, native 4K HDR and RAW support, and a faster, higher-quality distilled model.





 
 Can I fine-tune LTX-2.5 on my own data?
 
 



 Yes. LTX-2.5 includes a pretrained base checkpoint built specifically for fine-tuning, so you can adapt it to your own domain and deploy it on your own infrastructure.





 
 How fast is LTX-2.5?
 
 



 Self-hosted on 2× GB200 GPUs at 720p, LTX-2.5 generates a 10-second video clip in 6.8 seconds, which is faster than real time.





 
 What does the LTX-2.5 API cost?
 
 



 API pricing is billed per second of generated video. ltx-2-5-fast: $0.09/sec at 720p, $0.13/sec at 1080p, $0.19/sec at 1440p, and $0.30/sec at 4K. ltx-2-5-pro: $0.12/sec at 720p and $0.17/sec at 1080p. Full rates are on the API pricing page .





 
 What are the licensing terms for LTX-2.5?
 
 



 LTX-2.5 uses open weights with a permissive license, no mandatory branding, and free use under $10M ARR.
