 October 2, 2026

 LTX for Post-Production

 Open source



 
 Six new and upgraded capabilities give post-production teams more control over compositing, color, detail, and resolution, with outputs they can keep shaping throughout the pipeline.
 Built for Compositing 
 Alpha Gen: Generates alpha mattes with soft edges and fine detail, preserving hair, smoke, and partial transparency for compositing.
 Layout to Render: Generates a polished render from 3D blocking with a prompt or reference image, maintaining camera, composition, and frame alignment.
 Built for Color and Dynamic Range 
 HDR Native: Takes 16-bit EXR in and returns 16-bit EXR out through an ACES workflow, preserving the color and dynamic range of the input footage so output arrives ready to grade rather than flattened.
 SDR to HDR (Upgraded): Expands 8-bit SDR footage into 16-bit EXR in scene-linear ACEScg, ready to grade in a color-managed pipeline or encode for HDR displays.
 Built for Detail and Resolution 
 Native Resolution: Processes footage at full delivery resolution on a single GPU. VRAM consumption increases only slightly as resolution climbs.
 Refine & Restore: Improves clarity and detail in archival or low-quality footage. Combine with the Native Resolution workflow to reach 8K and beyond.

 LTX-2.3

 API



 
 Introducing LTX-2.3 — a major leap in quality and speed for AI video generation. LTX-2.3 is now the default model across the API, available in two variants:
 LTX-2.3 Pro ([.url-highlight]ltx-2-3-pro[.url-highlight]) — best-in-class quality, supports all endpoints including audio-to-video, retake, and extend.
 LTX-2.3 Fast ([.url-highlight]ltx-2-3-fast[.url-highlight] — blazing fast generation, supports text-to-video and image-to-video.
 What’s new
 Sharper fine details — new latent space with an updated VAE delivers noticeably crisper output.
 Cleaner audio — improved data filtering reduces background noise and artifacts.
 Stronger image-to-video — more natural motion, fewer static clips, and better visual consistency.
 Better prompt understanding — improved text connector architecture for closer adherence to complex prompts.
 Last-frame interpolation — provide a first and last frame to the image-to-video endpoint, and the model generates the video in between.
 Portrait video support — native 9:16 vertical video generation across all resolutions.
 24/48 FPS — new frame rate options alongside the existing 25/50 FPS.
